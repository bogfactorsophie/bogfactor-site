Bog Factor
Radio Vault Lore

For the curious.

Bog Factor is a radio show on EHFM hosted by Sophie and Emily. They play psychedelic folk, pagan boogie and sleaze, with detours into spiritual jazz and twisted bossa nova — digging out sounds from deep within the bog to bring merriment wherever they go.

They have been residents on EHFM since 2024. The show started as a monthly slot before moving to its current home on EHFM Mornings, every Monday from 10am to 12pm UK time. Sophie and Emily have also DJ'd at events in Edinburgh and Glasgow.

On the show they chat about folklore, Pictish stones, and weird local history, alongside a sharp focus on sound and music. Inspired by nature, folk and the neolithic, they take their name from the bogginess scale on walkhighlands.co.uk.
